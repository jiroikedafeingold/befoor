- Befoor is now Dingly. Same app, new name.
- Meeting notes: touch and hold a meeting and tap Add Note. Your note shows highlighted on its row, its alarm and the countdown — handy for follow-ups or things to bring up.
- Ring earlier for the meetings that need it: touch and hold a meeting and pick First Alert for just that meeting or its whole series, or set Keyword Alerts in Settings (for example, "kid pickup" rings 45 minutes before).
- Made for iPhone Duo: Dingly works on both screens and in any orientation, and its countdown fits the side Dynamic Island.
- Fixed: after you stopped the first alarm, the countdown sometimes didn't appear.
