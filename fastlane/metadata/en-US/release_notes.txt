Befoor 3.0 is a big update. It now requires iOS 26.

- Real alarms: meetings now ring with the same system alarms as the Clock app. They sound even when your iPhone is on silent or in a Focus, and Befoor no longer has to keep running in the background, which is much easier on your battery.
- Three alerts per meeting: 15, 7 and 1 minute before by default, each one adjustable in Settings.
- Snooze or stop right from the alarm. Stop skips that meeting's remaining alerts.
- New countdown to your next meeting in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, with its location, calendar and who it's with.
- Tidier notifications: only the newest one stays in Notification Center, and it clears itself after 30 minutes.
- Your calendar is checked for changes more often in the background.
- Updated onboarding and help to explain how it all works.
