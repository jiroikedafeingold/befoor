This update is all about battery life, without giving up the one thing Befoor promises: your alarm sounds on time even when the phone is locked and the ringer is off.

- Alarms now wake the phone only at the moment they need to ring, instead of checking the clock every second all day.
- The silent background stream that keeps alarms reliable now uses the lightest audio settings iOS allows.
- Notification banners for the same event are tidied up so a single meeting never leaves a stack of stale alerts.
- The widget refreshes only when the list of upcoming events actually changes.
- Calendar changes that arrive in bursts are handled with a single resync, and your other devices are only updated when the alarm list really changed.
- The haptic engine now powers down between alarms.
- Apple Watch: the People list only refreshes while it is on screen.
